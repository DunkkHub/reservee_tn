# Product Overview

## Purpose

Reservee TN is the modern booking platform for beauty businesses in Tunisia.

The product is designed around one sharp niche:

- barbers
- hair salons
- beauty centers
- nail studios
- spas

## Target users

### Customer

Customers want:

- fast discovery
- visible prices
- available times
- trusted businesses
- booking in under a minute

### Business owner

Business owners want:

- more bookings
- less WhatsApp chaos
- cleaner availability control
- fewer no-shows
- a premium online presence

### Platform admin

Admins want:

- strong moderation
- fake listing control
- trust and quality enforcement
- featured placement control
- visibility into bookings and business growth

## Product value

### Customer value

- Find a trusted nearby beauty business quickly.
- Compare services, prices, photos, and next available slots.
- Book without creating an account first.
- Manage a booking later with a reference code.

### Business value

- Show up with a premium public profile.
- Receive bookings in one structured system.
- Manage services, hours, blocked time, and gallery content.
- Operate with clearer booking statuses and fewer dead ends.

## MVP boundaries

Version 1 includes:

- public marketplace and discovery
- public business profile pages
- booking flow
- booking reference and manage-booking pages
- business dashboard
- admin moderation
- installable PWA shell

Version 1 excludes:

- customer accounts
- reviews
- loyalty
- deposits
- chain management
- advanced staff routing
- chat
- sports or medical workflows

## Version 1 booking model

- One shared calendar per business
- Service-level duration and price
- Opening hours plus blocked times
- Pending bookings that can expire automatically
- Manual business approval flow or instant booking mode

## Positioning

The platform should feel:

- premium
- modern
- calm
- trustworthy
- local without looking cheap

The core promise is simple:

- For customers: discover and book the best beauty appointments in Tunisia.
- For businesses: get booked, stay organized, and look premium online.
