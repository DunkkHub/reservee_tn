# Design System

## Visual direction

Reservee TN should feel:

- premium
- modern
- calm
- trustworthy
- feminine-neutral
- elegant without looking soft or childish

## Color system

### Core neutrals

- Background: `#0F1115`
- Card: `#151922`
- Elevated: `#1C2230`
- Primary text: `#F5F7FB`
- Secondary text: `#C7CFDA`
- Muted text: `#8E97A8`

### Accent colors

- Champagne gold: `#C8A96B`
- Soft rose warmth: `#D9A7A0`
- Success: `#3BB273`
- Warning: `#F0A202`
- Error: `#E15554`
- Info: `#4D9DE0`

### Usage rules

- 80% dark neutrals
- 15% text and light contrast
- 5% accent
- keep accent use intentional
- avoid over-coloring cards and badges

## Typography

- UI text: Inter
- Headings: Manrope or DM Sans

Recommended scale:

- Hero title: 40 to 52px
- Page title: 28 to 34px
- Section title: 22 to 26px
- Card title: 17 to 19px
- Body: 15 to 16px
- Small text: 12 to 13px

## Spacing and shape

- Radius: 14px cards, 12px inputs, pill buttons for status and tabs
- Spacing system: 4, 8, 12, 16, 24, 32, 48
- Keep generous air between blocks

## Surfaces

- Soft inner borders: `rgba(255,255,255,0.06)`
- Soft shadow: `0 10px 30px rgba(0,0,0,0.22)`
- Avoid harsh shadows or noisy gradients

## Components

### Buttons

- Primary button uses the gold accent
- Secondary button stays outlined or low-contrast
- Ghost button is reserved for lightweight actions

### Cards

- 16 to 20px padding
- soft border
- rounded corners
- hover lift on desktop only

### Badges and pills

Use for:

- business status
- booking status
- trust modules
- featured flags
- filters and tabs

### Inputs

- dark background
- strong focus ring in accent
- 48px minimum height
- clear placeholder text

## Motion

Use motion lightly:

- page fade or slide
- card hover lift
- slot selection response
- success confirmation transition

Avoid:

- heavy parallax
- long loading animations
- decorative motion that slows booking

## Photography

Prefer:

- clean interiors
- detailed work examples
- premium barber and salon atmosphere
- soft lighting
- Tunisian and North African representation where possible

Avoid:

- cheesy stock collages
- over-retouched glam shots
- low-resolution screenshots from social media
